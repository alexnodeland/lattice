# RFC-0001: v0.1 implementation plan

**Status:** Accepted
**Author:** Alex Nodeland
**Created:** 2026-10-12
**Discussion:** derived from [lattice RFC-0002][rfc-0002], accepted on 2026-10-12 with its decisions P1 to P6
**Siblings:**

- [lattice RFC-0002][rfc-0002] is the design this plan builds. Its decisions are portalr's [ADR-0001](../adr/0001-a-router-the-application-mounts.md) to [ADR-0006](../adr/0006-shared-stores-every-read-filtered.md).
- [lattice RFC-0001][rfc-0001] is grantr, whose API the UIs ask and the admin UI manages access through. Most of portalr's later phases wait on its phases.
- [stackr RFC-0003][s-rfc-0003] fixes portalr's layout, its Bun workspace and its CI, which RFC-0002 amends twice.

## Summary

portalr is the portal that lattice RFC-0002 designs: an `app` UI and an `admin` UI, as static single-page apps in React, sharing a typed `client` and a `ui` kit, and a router the application mounts that serves them and adds the few routes no library has. This RFC is portalr's plan for v0.1: RFC-0002's eight phases, after a foundation, with the parts each phase adds, where its work lands, and what it waits on. The design itself is RFC-0002's and is not repeated here.

## Motivation

RFC-0002 is a family document, and its phases reach into four packages besides portalr: grantr's API, artifactr's and reflexr's log records, and stackr's collector, stack and template. portalr needs a plan in its own series that its pull requests reference and tick, that says which work is portalr's, and what each phase waits on in the others.

## Design

### Parts

| Part | Holds | Phase |
|---|---|---|
| `packages/portalr/web/` | The Bun workspace's four packages, each a moon project with `lint`, `typecheck` and `check`, and `test` or `build` as it has code to test or build ([ADR-0008](../adr/0008-the-bun-workspace-runs-in-bun.md)) | 1 |
| `@portalr/ui` | The brand tokens and Base UI primitives (2); the chat and artifact components (5); the tables (6) | 1, 2, 5, 6 |
| `@portalr/client` | The generated types, the fetch client, and a WebSocket store per protocol, with its reducer | 1, 3 |
| `portalr.readers` | One reader per tool, Langfuse, Tempo, Loki, Prometheus and LiteLLM, each taking a `Tenant`, with the tests over every query | 4 |
| `portalr` (the router) | `portalr_router`: the UIs, `config.json`, the readers' routes, invites, and portalr's action catalog | 4 |
| `schemas/`, `bruno/` | portalr's OpenAPI document with its drift test, and its Bruno collection | 4 |
| `app` | Sign-in, the tenant and workspace pickers, and the app's areas | 1, 5 |
| `admin` | The admin's areas | 1, 6 |

A module's name may change when it is built; the architecture's status table shows what exists.

### Phases

| Phase | Deliverable | Lands in | Waits on | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Foundation | The package, its release, its site section, ADRs for P1 to P6, this plan and its tracking issue | portalr, lattice | Nothing | `portalr:check` and the site's strict build pass |
| 1. The workspace | The Bun workspace, the toolchains, Biome, TypeScript, knip and moon's TypeScript tasks; empty `client`, `ui`, `app` and `admin`; the TypeScript 7 check of each tool; osv-scanner and Renovate over `bun.lock` | portalr, lattice | Nothing | `moon ci` runs the web packages' `check` in CI, and an empty `app` builds |
| 2. The brand | [lattice ADR-0014](../../adr/0014-portalrs-and-grantrs-marks.md): portalr's and grantr's marks and working colours, and their tokens; `ui`'s tokens and primitives | portalr, lattice's brand | Nothing; drawn with the maintainer | `ui`'s components pass their browser tests in light and dark, and the site shows both new marks |
| 3. client | Generated types, the fetch client, and the WebSocket stores, tested against docplan and oncall | portalr | The OpenAPI documents above | A test client resumes a stream across a dropped connection without losing or repeating an event |
| 4. The router | `portalr_router`: serving the UIs, the readers and their filters, invites; its OpenAPI document and drift test; its Bruno collection; the tenant on log records, `tenant.id` in the collector, prom-label-proxy | portalr, artifactr, reflexr, stackr | grantr's phases 2 and 3 | The collection passes against an application on the stack, with each tool both up and down, and the readers' two-tenant test never sees the other tenant |
| 5. app | Sign-in, the tenant and workspace pickers, and the app's areas | portalr | grantr's phases 1 to 4, and its phase 5 for restricted items | Each area's `bdd` scenario passes |
| 6. admin | The admin's areas | portalr | grantr's phases 1 to 4 | Each area's `bdd` scenario passes, for a tenant admin and for an operator |
| 7. Template and acceptance | The `portal` question, the smoke test, the Acceptance job's portalr steps, and the size budgets | stackr's template, CI | Phases 4 to 6, and grantr's phase 4 | A generated application with a portal passes its CI, and the Acceptance job is green |
| 8. Docs and ADRs | portalr's guides, architecture and README, and the ADRs for each decision | portalr | Phases 1 to 7 | portalr's section builds, with the architecture updated |

Each phase is a series of small pull requests to `main`, and a phase brings its guides, reference pages, architecture and ADRs with it.

## Drawbacks

- **It waits on grantr.** Phases 4 to 7 can't finish until grantr's phases land, and grantr is planned in parallel.
- **Part of the plan lands elsewhere.** Phases 2, 4 and 7 change lattice's brand, artifactr, reflexr and stackr, so this checklist tracks work its own pull requests don't contain.

## Alternatives

RFC-0002 weighs portalr's design, and the alternatives to each decision, in its [Alternatives][rfc-0002-alternatives] and [Decisions][rfc-0002-decisions]. For the plan itself, tracking portalr's work in RFC-0002's checklist would keep one list, but a family one, which would mix portalr's progress with the other packages'.

## Unresolved questions

RFC-0002's own open questions stand: the working colours, spend by workspace, mobile, and analytics beyond usage and spend. One more moved:

- **Localization:** whether the UIs' strings go through a catalog from the start, or stay in English in the components until a second language is wanted. RFC-0002 gave it to phase 1, which built no screens. Settled in [ADR-0014](../adr/0014-the-apps-shell.md): English in the components, and a catalog with a second language.
