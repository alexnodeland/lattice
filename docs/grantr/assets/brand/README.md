# Brand

[lattice ADR-0014](../../../adr/0014-portalrs-and-grantrs-marks.md) records grantr's identity. It prints in yellow and key, since grants are keys, and its mark is a shield: artifactr's caret turned over, in key, with a slab across its top, in yellow. Where the slab crosses the arms, and where the arms cross at the foot, is its overprint, a dark gold, which is its working colour: yellow's own shade, rather than the near-black yellow and key would make. [`tokens.json`](tokens.json) holds its colours.

## Colours

| Name | Light | Dark | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Yellow | `#FFC400` | `#FFD54A` | The slab. Never text |
| Key | `#1C1D26` | `#8C8E9E` | The arms. On dark backgrounds, stackr's tint, so the overprint and the yellow stand apart from it |
| Dark gold | `#6B5300` | `#FFF6D6` | The overprint; the wordmark; the working colour |
| Paper | `#FAF8F0` | `#1A170C` | The banner's background |
| Graphite | `#625A45` | `#C4BBA3` | Secondary text, such as the banner's tagline |

Dark gold is 7.34:1 on white and 16.49:1 on the site's dark background, `#0D1920`; graphite is 6.44:1 and 9.37:1 on the papers.

## Known weaknesses

- **Its inverted triangle is near evalr's tick.** Both arms reach the top of the grid, where the tick cuts one short; it is symmetric, where the tick leans; and the slab closes it.
- **It borrows two siblings' shapes:** artifactr's caret, turned over, and stackr's slab.
- **On paper its overprint is lighter than the key it crosses,** where lattice's overprints are darker than both their inks.
